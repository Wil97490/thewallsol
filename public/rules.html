Sellable — and the badge says so

Soft flags

These don't stop a sale. They print under your ticker, publicly, for as long as you hold the seat.

A seat is held for — minutes while it is being paid for, and frees itself the second that runs out. Long enough to open a wallet and confirm; not long enough to park a seat you have no intention of buying.

You name the price

What a seat costs

There is no price list. The number under a ticker is what somebody actually paid for it.

SituationThe least you may pay
An empty seat$—
A seat somebody is sitting ontheir price, plus —% or $— — whichever is larger
Above thatwhatever you like, up to $—
A seat bought less than — minutes agonot for sale, at any price

The minimum increment is not there to take more of your money. It is there so a seat cannot be traded back and forth a cent at a time — which would earn the wall nothing, tell a reader nothing, and fill the hourly tape with noise.

A seat you buy is yours for — minutes, whatever anyone else offers for it. Nobody can outbid you out of a seat you paid for four minutes ago — not for twice the price, not for a hundred times it. After that, the seat is takeable again at the prices above.

Paying well above the minimum is the point of the mechanism, not an accident of it: the more you paid, the more it costs to take the seat from you, and the three most expensive seats get the large banners at the top of the wall. Size on this page is bought, and it is bought in public.

Our limits, stated as ours

When a check cannot run

A rule can only refuse you on something we actually measured. Everything else is our problem, and it is named as our problem.

SituationWhat happensWhy
the pool cannot be readsale held, retryAn unread pool is not an empty pool. We will not print $0 next to a token we failed to look at.
holders cannot be readsale held, retrySampling nobody is not the same as finding one wallet on the float.
safety check cannot runsale held, retryUnchecked is not malicious. It only means our checker did not answer.
the DEX is not modelledsellable, flaggedRetrying will not help — we simply cannot read that pool's lock yet. Refusing would punish a project for our blind spot, so the seat is sold with the gap printed on it. It never earns SCREENED.

Nothing in this table is ever written into the public refusal ledger. A refusal we publish is always something we measured about the contract — never something we failed to measure.

Contracts nobody sent us

We check tokens that never asked

Most of what the ledger holds was never submitted. We pick contracts that are buying attention somewhere else and run the same gate on them. If you would rather know before we look: the rules are all on this page, and they do not change for you.

How they are chosen. From public lists of projects paying for visibility — boosted listings, ads, claimed profiles. Not from tips, not from grudges, not from anyone paying us. A contract is checked once.

What runs. The identical rules above, on the identical facts, read from the chain. There is no separate, harsher screening for projects that did not buy a seat.

What gets published. Findings only — something we measured about the contract. A refusal that turns on a limit of ours is never published against anyone. Neither is a refusal that rests only on a link, because nobody handed us that link for this purpose.

What never gets published. A pass. If a contract nobody submitted clears every check, we say nothing at all — telling the world a token looks fine is a recommendation, and we do not make those about anything. The wall sells advertising space; it does not rate assets.

Every published check is worded so you can tell the two apart: refused at the gate means a buyer was turned away. not submitted — we ran the checks anyway means nobody asked, and no money was involved.

If your contract is in the ledger and a line is wrong, write to contact@thewallsol.com with the mint. A measurement we got wrong comes down, and the correction is public.

Read this before anyone sells you something

The Wall has no token

There is no $WALL. No presale, no allocation, no airdrop, no whitelist. Any token trading under this name is not ours, and anyone offering you one — in a reply, in a DM, in a group — is not us.

This is not modesty. A wall that screens tokens cannot also sell one: the moment we hold a bag, every refusal we publish becomes an accusation of self-interest, and the refusals are the only reason the badge means anything. So the answer is no, and it is structural rather than temporary.

If that ever changes, it will be announced here, on this page, and on our account — never first in a private message, never by someone asking you to send anything to secure a place.

The only payment this site will ever ask of you is the price of a seat, in SOL, to the treasury address displayed at checkout. There is no second address. If anyone tells you otherwise, write to contact@thewallsol.com and we will say so publicly.

What this is not

The part everyone skips

A badge means the checks above passed at the moment the seat was bought. It is not an audit, not an endorsement, and not a statement about what the token will do next. A team can pass every check on this page and still take your money — the checks close specific doors, they do not read intentions.

A seat is advertising space. Nothing on this site is investment advice.
